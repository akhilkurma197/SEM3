🛡️ Trust Layer Architecture & Design Philosophy

1. Pre-Action Defense

Traditional tools warn after a link is opened. Trust Layer intercepts links, QR payloads, and payment screenshots before action occurs.

2. Claim vs. Reality

Trust Layer checks whether what is presented matches reality — claimed brand vs. actual domain, claimed 12-digit UTR vs. actual numeric length.

3. Plain-Language Explainability

Instead of cryptic numbers, users receive clear explanations detailing why a threat was flagged and exactly what steps to take.
